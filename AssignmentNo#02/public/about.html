About this portal

A few notes on how we handle lost items on campus, and why this site exists.

flag Why we built it

People lose things at UET every week — student cards in the library, bottles in the cafeteria, laptops left in a lab after class. Notice boards get messy, and WhatsApp groups get buried. This site is a shared list so you can post once and others can search it later.

It was built for CS311 / CS224 Web Technologies in the Department of Computer Science. You can still use it like a real notice board: add a report, update it, or take it down when the item is back.

gavel Claiming an item

1. Prove it is yours

For wallets, phones, laptops, and ID cards, bring your UET student or staff card. Be ready to mention something that is not written on the listing (a sticker, a name inside the cover, the lock screen photo, etc.).

2. If you found something

Post it here, then leave the item at the nearest department office, the library counter, or campus security. Do not keep valuable items in your bag for days — the owner will look in those places first.

3. How long we keep things

Unclaimed items are usually held for about 90 days. After that, the department decides what to do with them. Mark the listing as Returned once the owner has collected it, so people stop looking.

help Common questions

I think I found my item on the list. What next?

Open the listing, note the location, and go there with your student card. After you collect it, ask someone to mark the status as Returned (or do it yourself from the details page).

How fast does a new post show up?

Straight away. As soon as you submit the form, it is saved and appears in Browse Items.

Can I change or delete a listing?

Yes. Use Edit on the card or the details page. Delete only if the post was a mistake or the item is no longer relevant.
